The Haunted House

You're locked in.

The front door slammed shut behind you. Thirteen doors in this house each hide one letter of the magic phrase that breaks the lock. Open a door, solve the problem behind it, and take its letter. When you have all 13, spell the phrase at the front door to escape.

  • The doors match the 13 problems on your 3-1 worksheet. You can open them in any order.
  • Stuck? Press Help. The first press gives a hint. More presses give more help, up to the full solution.
  • If you use More help or the full solution, you must solve a similar problem on your own to earn that door's letter.
  • When you escape you get a teacher code. Turn it in with your name, spelled the same way you type it here.
Choose your character

Walk with ←↑→↓ or WASD, or tap a spot on the map. Walk into a numbered door to open it. The front door is at the bottom.
